    InvoiceLogs

    Queryable, Introduced in version 2.55

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Sales/InvoiceLogs

    Represents an invoice log entry.

    GUID

    bd995956-dc78-4373-ba58-c79b638861c8

    Required permission

    Show customer orders

    Properties

    LoggingTimestamp DateTimeOffset The date when the log entry was created.
