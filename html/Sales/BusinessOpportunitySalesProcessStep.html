    BusinessOpportunitySalesProcessSteps

    Queryable, Introduced in version 25.9

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Sales/BusinessOpportunitySalesProcessSteps

    Represents a business opportunity sales process step.

    GUID

    f1b30770-5735-4607-a3d8-8a341ca15e4c

    Required permission

    Show business opportunity

    Properties

    Name Type Summary Introduced in
    Id Int64 The business key identifier of the business opportunity sales process step.
    Code Int64 The code of the sales process step.
    Description String The translated description of the sales process step.

    • Max length is 400
    Probability Int32 The probability of the sales process step.
    LeadTime Int32 The lead time of the sales process step.
    Responsibility Int32 Specifies what is responsibel for the sales process step.

    • Seller: 0
    • Group: 1
    ResponsibleSellerId Int64? The responsible seller of the sales process step.

    • References Persons
    ResponsibleSellerGroupId Int64? The responsible seller group of the sales process step.

    • References SellerGroups
    ResponsibleUserId Int64? The responsible application user of the sales process step.

    • References ApplicationUsers
    ParentId Int64? The business opportunity the sales process step belongs to.

    • References BusinessOpportunities
    RowIndex Int32? The row index of the sales process step.
    Status Int32 The status of the sales process step.

    • NotStarted: 0
    • Started: 1
    • Finished: 2
    IsActive Boolean Specifies if the sales process step is active.
