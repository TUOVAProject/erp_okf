    BusinessOpportunityActivities

    Queryable, Introduced in version 25.9

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Sales/BusinessOpportunityActivities

    Represents a business opportunity activity.

    GUID

    6525fbab-7229-4c61-a9ad-8d916f44b9e0

    Required permission

    Show business opportunity

    ActivityTypeId Int64? The business key identifier of the activity's type.
