    ShipmentCustoms

    Queryable, Introduced in version 26.4

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Sales/ShipmentCustoms

    Represents a shipments customs information.

    GUID

    628442CA-0EE8-4507-A749-96FE34D46B99

    Required permission

    Show shipment
