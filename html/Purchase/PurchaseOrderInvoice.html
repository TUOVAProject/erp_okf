    PurchaseOrderInvoices

    Queryable, Introduced in version 2.27

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Purchase/PurchaseOrderInvoices

    Represents a purchase order invoice entry

    GUID

    48914C07-4632-4904-8DAC-27BFA1E3FABC

    Required permission

    Show purchase orders

    Properties

    Name Type Summary Introduced in
    Id Int64 The business key identifier of the purchase order invoice.
    PurchaseOrderDeliveryId Int64? The business key identifier of the purchase order delivery.

    References PurchaseOrderDeliveries
    AccountGroupId Int64? The business key identifier of the supplier account group.

    References SupplierAccountGroups.
    WarehouseId Int64 The business key identifier of the warehouse that the purchase order invoice belongs to.

    References Warehouses
    VatGroupId Int64 The business key identifier of the VAT group that applies for the purchase order invoice.

    References VatGroups
    PaymentTermId Int64? The business key identifier of the payment term of the purchase order invoice.

    References PaymentTerms
    PaymentTerm PaymentTerms The payment term of the purchase order invoice.

    • Expandable
    PaymentTermDescription String The payment term description of the purchase order invoice.

    • Max length is 35
    GracePeriodInDays Int32? The purchase order invoice grace period in days.
    DeliveryMethodId Int64? The business key identifier of the purchase order invoice delivery method.

    References DeliveryMethods
    DeliveryTermId Int64? The business key identifier of the purchase order invoice delivery term.

    References DeliveryTerms
    DeliveryTermDescription String The delivery term description of the purchase order invoice.

    • Max length is 35
    DeliveryMethodDescription String The delivery method description of the purchase order invoice.

    • Max length is 35
    ShipmentPayer Int32 Specifies who is responsible for the shipment payment for the purchase order invoice.

    • BuyerPays: 1
    • SellerPays: 2
    • OtherPayer: 3
    • Incoterms: 4
    Rows PurchaseOrderInvoiceRows[] The rows of the purchase order invoice.

    • Expandable
