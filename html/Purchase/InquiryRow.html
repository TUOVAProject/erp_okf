    InquiryRows

    Queryable, Introduced in version 2.30

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Purchase/InquiryRows

    Represents an inquiry row.

    GUID

    1C7BE577-33A8-4CAF-9F52-16595EF161EB

    Required permission

    Show inquiry
