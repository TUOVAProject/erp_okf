    PurchaseOrderAdviceRowProductRecords

    Queryable, Introduced in version 22.2

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Purchase/PurchaseOrderAdviceRowProductRecords

    Represents a purchase order advice row product record.

    GUID

    6CBD9EF6-2FD4-44D4-B1D2-8568A451CFC8

    Required permission

    Show arrival

    SerialNumberCount Int32 Number of product records with serial number to create.
    ChargeNumber String The charge number of the product record(s).
