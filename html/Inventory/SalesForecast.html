    SalesForecasts

    Queryable, Introduced in version 22.3

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Inventory/SalesForecasts

    Represents a sales forecast.

    GUID

    eb703584-f436-4762-819c-2b1d65ee61f7

    Required permission

    Show basic data for part

    Properties

    Name Type Summary Introduced in
    Id Int64 The business key identifier of the sales forecast.
    ForecastCode String The human readbale identifier of the sales forecast.

    • Not nullable
    • Min length is 1
    • Max length is 8
    • Unique
    Description String The translated description of the sales forecast.
    WarehouseId Int64 The business key identifier of the warehouse that the sales forecast belongs to.

    • References Warehouses
    TimeSpan TimeSpan The interval time span of the sales forecast.
    IsActive Boolean The flag that indicates if the sales forecast is active.
    CustomerId Int64? The business key identifier for the customer of the sales forecast.

    • References Customers
    CustomerAccountGroupId Int64? The business key identifier for the customer account group of the sales forecast.

    • References CustomerAccountGroups
    CustomerDistrictId Int64? The business key identifier for the customer district of the sales forecast.

    • References CustomerDistricts
    SalesForecastRows SalesForecastRows[] The rows of the sales forecast.

    • Expandable
    		 Introduced in version 23.2
