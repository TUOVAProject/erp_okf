    AccountingBudgets

    Queryable, Introduced in version 23.3

    GET https://localhost:8001/sv/001.1/api/v1/Accounting/AccountingBudgets

    Represents an accounting budget.

    GUID

    646D760D-C81E-4B0A-90B1-6DD8C78F7B55

    Required permission

    Show budget

    Properties

    Name Type Summary Introduced in

    AccountYearSettingId Int64 The business key identifier of the account year setting the budget belongs to.

    AccountingYearId Int64 The business key identifier of the accounting year the budget belongs to.

    BalanceId Int64 The business key identifier of the budget's balance.

    BudgetChartId Int64 The business key identifier of the budget's budget chart.

    • References BudgetCharts
    BudgetTypeId Int64 The business key identifier of the budget type.

    • References AccountingBudgetTypes
    CodingEntryId Int64 The business key identifier of the budget's coding entry.

    • References CodingEntries
    CodingEntry CodingEntries The coding entry of the budget.

    Periods AccountingBudgetPeriods[] The periods of the accounting budget.
