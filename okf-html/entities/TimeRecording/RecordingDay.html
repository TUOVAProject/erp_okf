RecordingDays

Represents a Recording Day, which holds the attendance and work recordings which belongs to an employee for a specific date.

Properties

Property Type Null References Constraints Description
Id Int64 The business key identifier of the recording day.
RecordingDate DateTime The date this recording day belongs to.
PersonId Int64 Persons The business key identifier of the Employee that this recording day belongs to.
ScheduleId Int64? null The business key identifier of the workshop schedule assigned to this day. Null if recording without schedule.
OvertimeScheduleId Int64? null The business key identifier of the workshop overtime schedule assigned to this day. Null if recording without overtime schedule.
CalculatedScheduleDiff TimeSpan The calculated difference between the schedule length and the sum of recorded attendance and absence on the day.
CalculatedFlex TimeSpan The days calculated total flex-time.
CalculatedOvertime TimeSpan The days calculated total overtime.
CalculatedAbsence TimeSpan The days calculated total absence.
CalculatedAttendance TimeSpan The days calculated total attendance.
Exported Boolean Indicatates if the day has been exported by the salary export system. When true the creation of new or modification of existing attendance recordings is not allowed.
Authorized Boolean Indicatates if the day has been authorized.
AttendanceIntervals AttendanceIntervals[] AttendanceIntervals expandable, collection The recorded attendance intervals belonging to this day.
WorkIntervals WorkIntervals[] WorkIntervals expandable, collection The recorded work intervals belonging to this day.
Additions AdditionRecordings[] AdditionRecordings expandable, collection Additional recordings belonging to this day.
Approved Boolean since 22.1 Indicates that the employee is finished with the adding or adjustments.
CommentText String since 22.7 Recording comment
